Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · applies to the NeuroCalm AI app (com.kartikeyamishra.mobile) on Android and iOS

NeuroCalm AI ("NeuroCalm", "we") is built so that your most personal data stays on your device. This policy explains exactly what is processed, why, and your choices. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and app-store requirements.

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Journal entries, reflections, streaks, mood history and your weekly pattern report are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. We do not receive them.
  • Mood (face) scans are analysed on your phone. Images are never saved or uploaded.
  • If you allow Health/Health Connect access, sleep and step data is read on your phone and is not sent to us.

2. Data processed to provide the service

  • AI reflections. When you ask for a reflection, the text you type (or a short voice recording) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to Google's Gemini API to generate the reply. We do not store the text, the recording or the reply on our servers. Voice recordings are deleted from your phone right after processing.
  • Account. We create an anonymous account ID so your credits and purchases work. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Usage and credits. We keep your credit balance, daily usage counts, referral records and timestamps to run free allowances, credits and fraud protection.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we receive purchase status through RevenueCat, never your card details.
  • Safety. If text suggests a risk of self-harm, the app shows helpline numbers. We count how often this happens (a number only, no content).

3. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for consent through Google's consent tool, and on iOS through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices, or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. Premium users see no ads. See how Google uses data in ads.

4. Service providers

Google Firebase (hosting, authentication, database, cloud functions), Google Gemini API (AI replies), Google AdMob (ads), RevenueCat (purchase status), Google Play / Apple App Store (payments, installs). Data may be processed on servers outside India, including the United States, under these providers' safeguards.

5. Retention

Account, credit and usage records are kept while your account exists. Ad-reward and purchase-event bookkeeping is deleted automatically after 30–90 days. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account and cloud data in the app: Settings → Delete account & cloud data, or see how to request deletion.
  • Delete local journals: Settings → Wipe All Data, or uninstall the app.
  • Access, correction, withdrawal of consent and grievances: email kartikeyamishra099@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children

NeuroCalm is not intended for children under 13 (under 18 in India without a parent's consent). We do not knowingly process children's data.

8. Not medical advice

NeuroCalm is a self-help tool and not a medical device or a substitute for professional care. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

9. Changes and contact

We will post updates here and change the effective date. Grievance officer and contact: Kartikeya Mishra, Lucknow, India · kartikeyamishra099@gmail.com.
